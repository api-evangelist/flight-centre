# Flight Centre Travel Group

> Flight Centre Travel Group (ASX: FLT) is a Brisbane-headquartered travel retailer and corporate travel manager, one of the largest agency groups in the world and by far the largest in Australia. It runs more than thirty brands across leisure retail, corporate travel management and wholesale supply, with company-owned operations in twenty-four countries and licensed operations in roughly ninety more. It sits on the demand side of travel distribution: an aggregator-reseller that buys airline, hotel, cruise and land content through the GDSs (Sabre, Amadeus), through NDC aggregators, and through direct supplier agreements, and resells it to consumers and corporate travel programmes.

## API posture

Flight Centre Travel Group publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI or Swagger document, no GraphQL endpoint, no AsyncAPI, no webhook catalogue and no partner API documentation on any brand domain. Every developer-shaped subdomain probed on fctgl.com, fcmtravel.com, flightcentre.com.au, corporatetraveler.us, discova.com, studentuniverse.com and envoyage.com fails to resolve in DNS. There is no developer programme to apply to and no self-serve access gate.

The group is IATA NDC Level 4 certified as a travel management company (FCM states it was the first global TMC to reach that level, covering full offer and order management), but exposes no public NDC endpoint and publishes no NDC API.

The group's only genuine machine-readable distribution asset is TPConnects Technologies, the Dubai NDC aggregator in which Flight Centre holds a 70% stake and which is listed as a group brand. Its Iris (travel-seller side) and Astra (airline side) APIs implement IATA NDC Schema 18.2 and 21.3 across 60+ NDC/LCC carriers and 4 GDSs, and TPConnects announced MCP layers over both platforms in March and April 2026 — but the reference documentation sits behind a ReadMe/Auth0 login and the API host api.iris.tpconnects.com returns 401 for every path. Nothing there is publicly consumable.

- [TPConnects Astra API](https://tpconnects.com/astra-airline-solutions/astra-api/): Airline-side NDC gateway. Demo-request access only.
